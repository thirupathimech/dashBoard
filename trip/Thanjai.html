01. Pick-up at Trichy

Begin with the overnight drive from Coimbatore and meet your pickup at Trichy at dawn. Keep a light blanket, water and a small travel bag handy.

📍 Trichy⏱ 30 min planned stop

02. Brihadeeswarar Temple

Visit Thanjavur’s monumental Big Temple, celebrated for its Chola architecture, towering vimana and intricate stonework. Early morning is ideal for a calmer visit.

🏛 Heritage⏱ 3.5 hr visit window
10:30 AM → 11:00 AM · 30 min transfer

03. Thanjavur Palace

Explore the palace complex and its historic atmosphere. Check which galleries and sections are open on the day, as access and timings can vary.

👑 Royal history⏱ 1 hr 30 min visit
12:30 PM → 2:30 PM · 2 hr

04. Drive to Nagore + lunch

Head towards Nagore and make time for lunch along the way. A relaxed meal and a short water break will help you enjoy the afternoon stops.

🚗 Road transfer🍽 Lunch break
4:00 PM → 4:30 PM · 30 min transfer

05. Nagore Beach

Pause by the coast for sea air and a gentle walk. Keep an eye on the time so you can reach the Dargah before your planned evening visit.

🌊 Coastal pause⏱ 1 hr planned
5:00 PM → 5:30 PM · 30 min transfer

06. Nagore Dargah

Spend time at the revered shrine, known for welcoming visitors from many backgrounds. Dress respectfully and follow local guidance on photography and visitor areas.

07. Arrive at Velankanni

Drive to Velankanni, collect dinner on arrival and settle in for the night. Rest well — tomorrow’s plan includes the Basilica and the beach before the return journey.
